From the desk to the display

Run the order in Orion. The screens in the church show the image or the reading.

Built for a church service

Service order

Keep the welcome, offering, and closing in folders, then send each image when it is time.

Hymns

Open a hymnal, move through the stanzas, and project the one the congregation is singing.

Bible

Find a passage, show the reference, and follow cross references without leaving the page.

Presentations

Bring in slides from the files the speaker already has, including PDF and Keynote.

Media

Play video and open a web page on the output, with the operator view kept separate.

Libraries

Download church content inside the app. SDA Panorama Church is one library, and more can be added.
